# Hub-Terra

**Geospatial science and environmental solutions for biodiversity, conservation and land management.**

[Hub-Terra](https://www.hub-terra.es/) is an environmental research and consulting company based in Málaga, Spain. We combine ecological knowledge, geospatial analysis, modelling and environmental planning to turn scientific evidence into practical tools for decision-making.

## What we do

- **Biodiversity and conservation:** species and habitat assessments, invasive species, conservation priorities and management strategies.
- **GIS, remote sensing and spatial modelling:** geospatial data analysis, ecological modelling, mapping and decision-support tools.
- **Ecosystem services and environmental indicators:** assessment, monitoring frameworks and evaluation of management outcomes.
- **Environmental planning and governance:** wetland and coastal management, restoration planning, technical assessments and participatory processes.

## Research, code and data

This GitHub organization hosts selected code, reproducible workflows and technical documentation developed through our research and applied projects.

We aim to make methods transparent and reusable while respecting data provenance, quality requirements, licensing terms and the limitations of biodiversity observations. Materials are released publicly when appropriate and after validation; some project repositories remain private during development.

## Work with us

We collaborate with research groups, public administrations, conservation organizations and multidisciplinary teams in Spain and internationally.
